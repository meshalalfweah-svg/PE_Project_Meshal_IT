# IT-Rules-v1_2026_10_15

Owner: Meshal Hayaf Alfweah · Version: v1 · Date: 2026_10_15

These five rules adapt the Day 4 rules to the Internal IT Help Desk. They govern every template; fixed safety rules are also embedded in each template.

| Area | Rule | Yes/no check |
|---|---|---|
| Data | Before AI input, the operator masks names, usernames, emails, phone numbers and device codes, and removes passwords, verification codes, ID numbers and bank details. Use invented data for training. | Is the input invented and masked, with unnecessary sensitive details removed? |
| Verification | Before any reply is sent, the technician checks every service claim and number against the current Facts block, checks employee-reported content against the ticket, reviews CHECK, and requires all five output checks to pass. | Are all claims supported, exact, safe, complete and natural? |
| Fairness | The owner compares equivalent tickets written formally and informally before using a template and after changes; service standards, referral and approval requirements must stay equal. | Was the pair reviewed, with no unjustified difference? |
| Policy | Use only an AI tool approved for the organization. Record template version, date, tool and actual reviewer before operational use. A training self-assessment is not workplace approval. | Are tool authorization and the actual review record complete before workplace use? |
| Human decision | A technician or responsible manager decides approvals, access changes, exceptions and ticket outcomes. The assistant drafts only, and never claims to reset, approve, purchase, route or resolve anything. | Did the responsible person decide and perform the action? |
